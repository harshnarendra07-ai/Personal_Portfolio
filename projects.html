01

End-to-end sales analytics

Personal project · Dec 2025

PythonPostgreSQLPower QueryPower BIDAX

I wanted to take raw transaction data all the way to a dashboard a manager could use, and do each step properly.

What I did

  • Wrote a Python pipeline to clean the raw datasets and fill in missing values, so the reporting could be trusted.
  • Loaded the cleaned data into PostgreSQL and reconciled it using window functions and CTEs.
  • Modelled it in Power BI as a star schema: one sales fact table with customer, product, date and region dimensions, one-to-many relationships and single-direction filters.
  • Used DAX variables and removed redundant columns and relationships to keep the report fast.

What it showed

  • A small share of customers brings in a large share of total revenue.
  • Returning customers spend more on average than new ones.

02

WeCanCIC community platform

The Cloud Crew CIC · client work · 2026

ReactTypeScriptSupabaseAgileGDPR

WeCan collects empty aluminium cans, sells them to scrap yards and uses the money to buy food for people in need. My team first worked with them at the Automation for Charities AI Hackathon, which we won. As interns at The Cloud Crew, we then built the platform.

The problem

The charity had no central place for volunteer sign-ups, community discussion or events.

What we built

  • Gathered requirements with the client and delivered an MVP on a Supabase backend: volunteer sign-up, a discussion forum, an FAQ chatbot for volunteers and points-based recognition.
  • Released page by page in weekly sprints for client review and sign-off. I learned React and TypeScript in a month to build the front end.
  • Applied the Data Protection Act and GDPR to 300+ users' data, storing volunteer locations at city level unless the user is signed in.

Outcome

We handed over a platform the client maintains themselves. They report more volunteer engagement and wider social reach after SEO work and social media integration.

03

Sky engineering directory

University of Westminster · team lead · Sep 2025 – May 2026

DjangoPythonSQLBootstrap 5Trello

A corporate engineering directory and meeting-scheduling web app for Sky, built by a 6-person Agile team over 12 weeks and presented to a panel of Sky engineers. I led the team and was technical lead for the database, integration and security.

What I did

  • Database: designed the relational structure and wrote the full logical ERD. Wrote an import_data.py script to generate teams and accounts, and scripted local test databases so the team stopped hitting merge conflicts.
  • Backend: built the meeting-coordination logic, with triggers that send live alerts to teams when an event is created.
  • Security: Django authentication with PBKDF2/SHA256 password hashing, @login_required on protected routes, and tests for CSRF tokens and edge-case inputs. Tightened .gitignore rules to stop sensitive files being exposed.
  • Front end: forms, profiles and login pages in Bootstrap 5, with visible focus states and high-contrast layouts for accessibility.
  • Delivery: ran sprints and risks on Trello, and managed the Git repository, branches and merges.

06

Trazer: UK Parliament Hackathon

EasyA · finalist · Sep 2026

ResearchPitchingLLMsRAG

Our team was picked as one of the top 100 from over 1,000 applicants to pitch inside the Houses of Parliament.

Parliament records what ministers promise but doesn't track whether it happens. Trazer pulls commitments out of Hansard debates and written answers, looks for later evidence, and marks each one as fulfilled, in progress or no evidence found, with a source link.

I researched the idea and built and presented the pitch deck; a teammate built the data pipeline.
